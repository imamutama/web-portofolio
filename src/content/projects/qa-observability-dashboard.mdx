## Problem

The QA team lacked a single view of automation health, and test data was scattered across the team, slowing everyone down.

## Approach

Produced visual aids for the QA team to monitor and refine automation processes, improving overall testing visibility. Created and maintained test libraries that simplify access to test data for QA team members.

## Result

- Improved visibility into automation process health.
- Centralized, easier access to test data.
